Biography

I work as an Economist for Deutsche Bank in London where I write research on a range of macroeconomic topics that affect the European Central Bank and the countries of the Euro Area.

I completed my Ph.D. in economics at the University of Chicago where my thesis focuses on productivity knowledge spillovers and labour mobility. I also have central-banking experience working at the Reserve Bank of New Zealand.
