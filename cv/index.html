Experience

European Economist

Deutsche Bank

Sep 2018 – Present

Conducting economic research to improve our understanding of issues related to ECB monetary policy and euro-area economies.

Developing and maintaining a variety of models and frameworks that feed into the research team’s views and predictions on ECB monetary policy decisions. Notable model frameworks include the shadow analysis framework for measuring the ECB’s policy stance accounting for unconventional monetary policy and interest rates constrained by the lower bound, and the analysis of dbDIG household inflation expectations.
